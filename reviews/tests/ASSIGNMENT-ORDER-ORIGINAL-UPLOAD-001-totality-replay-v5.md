observe the application calling those dependency ports. Instead, the storage
fake directly calls the shared test helper from each of its own operations:

```php

$this->owner->trip('storage_observer');
```

Those calls occur in `beginStage`, `write`, `completedBytesForInspection`,
`finalize`, `abort` and `close`; they bypass the injected
`AssignmentOrderOriginalFaultInjector::before()` and
`AssignmentOrderOriginalStorageObserver::observe()` implementations. A
plausible implementation can therefore omit every real fault-injector and
storage-observer call while the fake storage alone supplies calls 1–5 and makes
the matrix pass. The fixture is attesting to its own storage choreography, not
to the application's port totality or exact observer positions required by v4.

positions. It does not pin the exact reason/retryable/evidence tuple, storage
cleanup trace, or which typed lifecycle/storage/fault event reached the port.

observer to the wrong failure family can remain undetected even after replacing
the self-generated counters.
